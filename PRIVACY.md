# Privacy policy — Quiet Steps

Last updated: 18 October 2026 (app version 1.0.3)

Published at https://web.wainshop.com/quiet-steps-privacy (also linked on About & credits in the app).

Quiet Steps counts steps on your phone. It does not need an account and it has no internet access. Made by Wain. Contact: [contact email].

## What the app stores (on your device only)

- Today’s step count and recent daily totals
- The numbers you type: height, stride, weight (kcal estimate only), daily goal
- Walks you record: mood, steps, minutes, optional note
- Settings: language, look, voice, reminders, quiet hours
- A copy of a background photo, if you choose one

All of this stays in the app’s private storage on your device.

## What the app does not do

- No internet permission
- No GPS, no location
- No account
- No advertising
- No analytics
- No upload of step or health data
- No sharing with other companies

## Photos, postcards, backups

- Background photo: chosen in Android’s system photo picker; the app gets only the photo you pick and asks for no photo-library permission.
- Postcards: drawn on the phone; saved to Pictures/Quiet Steps only when you tap “Save to phone”; shared only when you tap Share and pick an app.
- Backup: a JSON file you export and place yourself (no photos, no device IDs). Import reads only the file you pick.
- If Android device backup is on, Android may include app data in your Google backup; Wain never receives it.

## Permissions

- Physical activity — so the phone sensor can count steps
- Notifications — counting notification, milestone messages, optional evening reflection
- Foreground service (health) and start at boot — counting continues in the background and after a restart
- Storage, Android 8–10 only — saving a postcard to the gallery on those versions

## Medical

Quiet Steps is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Distance and calories are estimates only.
