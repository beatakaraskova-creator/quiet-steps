# Privacy policy — Quiet Steps

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Quiet Steps counts steps on your phone. It does not need an account and it does not need the internet.

## What the app stores

- Today’s step count and recent daily totals
- The numbers you type: height, stride, weight, daily goal

All of this stays in the app’s storage on your device.

## What the app does not do

- No GPS
- No location
- No account
- No advertising
- No analytics
- No upload of step or health data
- No sharing with other companies

## Permissions

- Physical activity — so the phone sensor can count steps
- Notifications — so counting can continue when the app is in the background
- Nothing else is required

## Medical

Quiet Steps is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Distance and calories are estimates only.
